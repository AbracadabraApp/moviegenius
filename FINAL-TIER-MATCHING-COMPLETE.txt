## Key Learnings

### Why Manual TMDB ID Collection Was Necessary
1. **TMDB text search favors popularity** - "X" returns "Fast X", "Eclipse" returns "Twilight"
2. **Translation/alternate titles** - "Hana-bi" stored as "Fireworks", "Quai des Orfèvres" as "Jenny Lamour"
3. **Regional titles** - "Mad Max 2" (Australia) vs "The Road Warrior" (US)
4. **Year mismatches** - TMDB release dates sometimes differ by 1 year from production year

### What Worked
- ✅ Database fuzzy matching for 118 films (year ±2 tolerance)
- ✅ Manual TMDB ID lookup for obscure/foreign/old films
- ✅ Batch processing with rate limiting (250ms between requests)
- ✅ Script-generated Swift dictionary entries (no typos)

### What Didn't Work
- ❌ Raw TMDB text search without year filtering (too many wrong matches)
- ❌ Assuming TMDB movie API works for TV series (it doesn't)
- ❌ Relying on exact title matches for international films
