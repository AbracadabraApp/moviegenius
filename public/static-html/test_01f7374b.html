Versailles follows the young King Louis XIV's ambitious vision to transform his father's hunting lodge into the most magnificent palace in Europe, while consolidating absolute power in 17th century France. The series masterfully weaves political machinations, romantic entanglements, and court intrigue as Louis outmaneuvers nobles who resist his centralization of power. The show's narrative structure echoes the complex political maneuvering of The Crown (2016), while maintaining its own distinct historical personality.
George Blagden delivers a nuanced performance as Louis XIV, capturing both the king's ruthless ambition and private vulnerabilities. The series benefits from cinematic production values that rival films like Marie Antoinette (2006), with meticulous attention to period detail in costumes and set design. Alexander Vlahos brings dramatic depth to Philippe, Louis's brother, whose complex relationship with the king drives many of the show's most compelling moments.
The series explores themes of power, modernization, and the birth of absolute monarchy that resonate with contemporary discussions about centralized authority and social change. Like The Favourite (2018), it examines the personal costs of wielding power and the isolation of leadership. The show's treatment of sexuality and gender roles, particularly through Philippe's character, offers a surprisingly modern lens on historical figures.
Versailles elevates the historical drama genre by combining the political complexity of Wolf Hall (2015) with the visual grandeur of The Great (2020). It stands apart from traditional period dramas by embracing a more contemporary storytelling style, using modern music and editing techniques while maintaining historical authenticity.
The series resonates with modern audiences through its exploration of power consolidation, media manipulation, and political branding - themes that echo in shows like House of Cards (2013). Its examination of how Louis XIV created the celebrity culture of his time draws fascinating parallels to contemporary social media and influence.
