Anna Foster, the President's daughter, yearns for freedom from her overprotective Secret Service detail. During a diplomatic trip to Prague, she escapes her handlers and meets Ben Calder, a charming British stranger who becomes her companion on a whirlwind European adventure. Unknown to Anna, Ben is actually a Secret Service agent assigned to protect her, creating the central dramatic irony that drives the narrative. The film attempts to recreate the magic of classic romantic comedies like Roman Holiday (1953), but with a modern twist.
Mandy Moore brings her characteristic sweetness to Anna, though the role doesn't stretch her acting abilities significantly. Matthew Goode makes his Hollywood debut as Ben, showing early glimpses of the charm that would later serve him well in films like Stoker (2013). The chemistry between the leads is pleasant but lacks the spark that elevated similar films like Notting Hill (1999) or The Princess Diaries (2001).
Released during a period when teen-oriented romantic comedies were evolving beyond high school settings, 'Chasing Liberty' attempts to bridge the gap between youth and adult romance. It follows in the footsteps of films like What a Girl Wants (2003) and precedes similar presidential daughter narratives like First Daughter (2004), reflecting Hollywood's fascination with stories about privileged young women seeking normalcy.
The film sits within the early 2000s romantic comedy tradition, when the genre was moving away from the sophisticated approach of films like You've Got Mail (1998) toward more youth-oriented fare. It shares DNA with other European-set romance films like The Lizzie McGuire Movie (2003), capitalizing on audiences' appetite for escapist travelogue elements combined with love stories.
While themes of privacy, freedom, and the challenges of growing up in the public eye remain relevant, the film's treatment feels dated compared to modern explorations of fame and privilege. Modern audiences might find more depth in films like The American President (1995) or Long Shot (2019) for political romance, or To All the Boys I've Loved Before (2018) for young adult romance.
