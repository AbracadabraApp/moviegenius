The documentary follows Jeremy Scott's remarkable journey from a small-town farm in Missouri to becoming the creative director of Moschino. Through intimate interviews and behind-the-scenes footage, we witness Scott's transformation from an outsider rejected by the fashion establishment to one of the industry's most influential voices. The film captures his preparation for his first Moschino collection, while weaving in his personal history and rise to prominence through sheer determination and unique creative vision.
Scott emerges as a charismatic subject, openly sharing his vulnerabilities and triumphs. Celebrity interviews with supporters like Katy Perry, Miley Cyrus, and A$AP Rocky provide context for his cultural impact. The documentary's strength lies in its ability to showcase Scott's artistic process, from initial sketches to runway presentations, while maintaining focus on the human story behind the fashion. Director Vlad Yudin captures both the glamorous and grueling aspects of the fashion world with equal attention.
The film explores themes of democratizing fashion and challenging industry elitism, similar to themes seen in The September Issue (2009) and McQueen (2018). Scott's approach to fashion as entertainment and his embrace of pop culture elements mirrors the cultural shift documented in Bill Cunningham New York (2010). His story represents the changing landscape of fashion in the social media age, where traditional gatekeepers hold less power.
Within the fashion documentary genre, this film stands alongside Dior and I (2014) and The First Monday in May (2016) in its exploration of creative leadership in prestigious fashion houses. However, it distinguishes itself by focusing on a designer who intentionally breaks from tradition and embraces mass culture, more akin to the rebellious spirit seen in Westwood: Punk, Icon, Activist (2018).
Scott's vision of fashion as inclusive entertainment rather than exclusive luxury remains highly relevant in today's discussions about democratization of style and breaking down traditional barriers. His influence on contemporary pop culture and fashion continues to grow, making this documentary an important record of a pivotal moment in fashion history.
