How Do I Look provides an intimate look into New York City's ballroom scene, following various house mothers and their chosen families as they navigate the competitive world of voguing and ball culture. The documentary captures the evolution of this underground scene through the 1990s and early 2000s, showcasing the artistry, drama, and personal struggles of its participants. Through raw and honest interviews, we meet legendary figures like Kevin Ultra Omni and Carmen Xtravaganza, who share their experiences of building community through adversity.
Director Wolfgang Busch takes a respectful, observational approach, allowing the subjects to tell their own stories without unnecessary editorial intervention. The film's strength lies in its authentic portrayal of ball culture personalities, capturing both the glamorous performances and the challenging realities of their daily lives. The documentary excels in showing how these performers create art and family in the face of societal marginalization.
Building on the groundwork laid by Paris Is Burning (1990), this film documents the continuing evolution of ballroom culture and its influence on mainstream fashion and dance. It explores themes of identity, chosen family, and survival, while highlighting how the community adapted to changes in the early 2000s. The film serves as a crucial historical document of LGBTQ+ culture and resistance through artistic expression.
Within the realm of LGBTQ+ documentaries, How Do I Look follows the tradition of intimate community portraits like The Queen (1968) and Paris Is Burning (1990), while predating mainstream explorations like Strike a Pose (2016) and Kiki (2016). Its raw, direct approach influenced later documentaries about underground culture and performance art.
The film's themes of identity, community, and artistic expression remain deeply relevant today, especially as ball culture has entered mainstream consciousness through shows like Pose (2018) and Legendary (2020). It serves as an important historical bridge, documenting how the culture evolved and survived through different eras.
